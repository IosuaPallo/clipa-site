How it works

Three steps, then it disappears.

  1. 01

    Pick your apps

    The ones you open without deciding to. Most people start with one and know exactly which.

  2. 02

    Write your reason

    One line, in your own words. You will read it back at the exact moment it is relevant, which is the only moment it works.

  3. 03

    Open the app anyway, or don’t

    There is always a way through. A lock you resent is a lock you uninstall, so Clipa never becomes one.

Why a sentence, and not a countdown

A spinning ring can be watched with unfocused eyes. You learn its shape in a day and then you are just waiting it out.

An unfinished sentence cannot be skimmed. Until the last word lands there is nothing to hold, so you read it — and reading your own reason is the whole mechanism.

A ring

Learned in a day. Watchable without reading.

A sentence

Cannot be parsed until it is finished.
