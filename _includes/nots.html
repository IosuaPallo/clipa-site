What it does not do

There is no account, because there is nothing to store.

Clipa sees which app came to the foreground and for how long. It never sees what is inside one, and none of it leaves the phone.

There is no server to sign in to and nothing for anyone to lose. Your stats live in your pocket and nowhere else. Export all of it whenever you like, or delete all of it in one tap.
