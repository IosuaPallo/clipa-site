Same inversion as the hero: with no JS the band renders already dark, which is the state that makes the point, rather than a light band that never changes. 700ms is deliberately slower than any duration in the app. It stands in for a change of hour, not a UI response, and everything transitions together so it reads as one event rather than a dozen. {%- endcomment -%}

After ten

At one in the morning, a white screen is not a neutral act.

Between 22:00 and 06:00 the pause changes its own colours — warm amber on near-black, no blue — and it turns your screen brightness down while it is on top. It never turns it up.

This is the hour the app was built for. It is also the hour most apps stop caring.

  • Warm palette, no blue
  • Brightness lowered, never raised
  • Never a sound
