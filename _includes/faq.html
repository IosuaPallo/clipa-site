Questions

The ones worth asking.

If something is missing, write in. A person reads it.

Does it actually work, or is it another app I will delete?

It works while the pause still makes you read. That is why the sentence is your own words and why the prompts rotate — a fixed screen becomes wallpaper in a week. Clipa is a few seconds of friction placed exactly where the reflex lives; it is not therapy and it does not pretend to be.

Can I get past it?

Always, on every pause, with one tap. That is deliberate. An app you cannot get out of is an app you uninstall in a bad moment, and then it helps you never again.

What can Clipa see?

Which app came to the foreground, and for how long. That is the whole list. It cannot see inside an app — not your messages, not what you scrolled, not who you looked at — and it has nowhere to send anything even if it could.

Will it drain my battery?

It stops looking entirely while your screen is off, which is most of the day, and it holds no wake locks. The target is under one percent of a day.

Why does it need permission to draw over other apps?

Because that is literally how the pause appears on top of the app you just opened. Android treats it as a special permission and asks you plainly, which is right. Clipa draws its own screen and never reads what is underneath.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 11 and up. Some phones — Xiaomi and Samsung especially — kill background apps aggressively, so Clipa checks whether it is still running and tells you how to fix it on your specific phone rather than failing silently.
