There is no form, because there is no backend and this is not the product to wire a third-party list into: a page whose argument is that nothing about you leaves your phone should not hand your address to a service in order to say so. So it is a mailto:. The honest consequence is that the promise gets stronger rather than weaker - the design's small print said "one email, then the list is deleted", and now there is no list at all to delete. That is a claim that cannot rot, which the other version could. Colours are the app's own dark pair rather than an invented palette, so the card reads as Clipa's dark mode: --ink-050 on --text, with --green-400 for the action. {%- endcomment -%}
